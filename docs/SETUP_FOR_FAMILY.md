# Getting your family’s Home Base ready

Follow [Cloudflare setup for Matt](CLOUDFLARE_SETUP_FOR_MATT.md). It contains the dashboard steps for the existing Cloudflare account and MDhomebase.com. No Terminal commands or separate database/storage accounts are needed.

The cottage, magic word and immediate Mom/Dad/Mia selection stay the same. Profiles have no passwords. Choose your own name so notes, favorites and private clues belong to you. Remembering a device lasts up to 30 days; uncheck it on a shared device and sign out when finished.

Messages, Family Board and five Mystery Club cases are implemented. Photos, recordings and drawings use private R2. Saved notes, boards and games use D1. Our Story remains a placeholder. No one’s location, online status or last visit is tracked.

After the live HTTPS website is ready, open it on the family devices. On iPad Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen. The offline page is generic and never exposes cached family content.
